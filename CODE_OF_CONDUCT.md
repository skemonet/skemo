# Code of conduct

skemo adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In practice

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness, respect for differing
opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for our mistakes.

Unacceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without their explicit permission,
and any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere. Report unacceptable behaviour to **claude@skemo.net**; reports are reviewed
promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently
ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban).
