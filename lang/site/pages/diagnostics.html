Errors and warnings

skemo check and every render report problems with the file, the line, the column, a caret under the culprit and, often, a hint.

error[unknown-target]: unknown target « b »
  ┌─ diagram.skemo:2:6
  │
2 │   to(b) go
  │      ^
  = help: did you mean « a »?

Errors stop the rendering (exit code 1). Warnings never do, unless --deny-warnings is given. Codes are stable: you can rely on them in scripts. Messages follow the system language.

multiple-attr-groupstwo pairs of parentheses on one tag

bad-numbermalformed at, size or via
bad-marker, multiple-appearmalformed animation marker (@0, @4-2, unknown effect), or two appearance markers on one element
legacy-and-markerold step= notation and an @ marker on the same element
unsafe-hrefa link with a forbidden scheme, or empty
missing-date, bad-date, end-before-starttimeline and frieze dates (including year 0)
zone-in-frise, zone-in-mindmapzones in a mode that does not support them
bad-rootmind map root that is not a card id

CodeCause

unknown-attributediagram(engine=…), router=… or elk=…: options of the prototype, no longer read
bad-table (preview)a table row that cannot be read as written: ^^ in the first row, a row starting with ||, a separator out of place or with another number of columns, a one-cell table between lines of text (write \| for a final bar)
bad-at (preview)at= that is neither coordinates nor a compass point, or a compass point on an element that is not at the top level
docked-arrow (preview)an arrow between a docked element (at="s"…) and the rest of the drawing: not drawn

The engine adds a few warnings of its own: two pinned cards that overlap (both are named), an animation marker earlier than its parent allows, a mind map card that the root cannot reach, a contrast below the threshold with --contrast.
