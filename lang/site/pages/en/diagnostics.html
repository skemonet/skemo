Errors and warnings

skemo lint and every render report problems with the file, the line, the column, a caret under the culprit and, often, a hint.

error[unknown-target]: unknown target `b`
  ┌─ diagram.skemo:2:6
  │
2 │   to(b) go
  │      ^
  = help: did you mean `a`?

Errors stop the rendering (exit code 1). Warnings never do, unless --deny-warnings is given. Codes are stable: you can rely on them in scripts. Messages follow the system language.

Warnings of the stylesheets

They point at the line of the .css file.

CodeCause
css-propertya typo of a property skemo or CSS knows: colr, --lable (the message suggests the right name). Your own variables and any valid CSS are left alone
css-syntaxa { never closed, a } without {, a declaration outside a rule or without :

With --contrast, a contrast below the threshold is reported too.
