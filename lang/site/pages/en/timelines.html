Timelines and roadmaps

For roadmaps, project plans and release schedules: a card's position comes from its dates, top-level zones become lanes, and arrows are dependencies.

Options

Dates

YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD. end is inclusive: end="2027-03" runs to 31 March. A start given as a month means its first day. Every card needs date or start; end earlier than start is an error.

Month and quarter labels follow the language of the messages; pin them with diagram(lang="en") for a file that must render identically everywhere.

Lanes and arrows

  • Top-level zones are lanes, in writing order; cards outside any zone form an unframed lane at the top.
  • Cards that overlap in time stack on successive rows of their lane.
  • Arrows are dependencies: they leave on the right and arrive on the left within a lane, freely across lanes.
  • at= has no effect; animation markers work as usual (the axis diamond follows its milestone).
