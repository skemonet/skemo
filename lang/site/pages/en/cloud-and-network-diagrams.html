Cloud and network diagrams

Regions, networks and subnets are nested zones; machines are cards, a group of identical instances is a pile. Address ranges, ports and protocols go in chips and labels. skemo keeps every arrow clear of the boxes it does not join.

Tips

  • Write zones from the outside in (region, network, subnet) and put each card in the deepest zone it belongs to.
  • Two zones linked both ways (the load balancer to the app, the app out through the NAT) keep the order they are written in. Another arrow that goes back up (a return path, monitoring) can still push the layout around: give it constraint="false".
  • Several instances of one thing are one pile, not several cards: the drawing stays as wide as the idea.
  • For a page about one network, put a legend of the flow kinds in a corner: card.legend(at="ne").
