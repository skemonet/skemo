Organisation charts and trees

A hierarchy is cards and the arrows from each parent to its children. With join="trunk", the arrows of one parent share a trunk and branch out, the way organisation charts are drawn.

How it is written

  • diagram(dir="down"): the root on top, each level one row lower.
  • to(cto cfo cpo join="trunk") under the root draws one trunk to its three reports. Each report still has its own arrow, so it can have its own style or animation marker.
  • Children keep the order you wrote them in. A parent is centred over its children.
  • A type per level (.lead, .manager, .team) colours the levels and writes their category above the name.

Name your types after what they mean (.manager, .team). A few class names belong to the drawing itself and would style a part of every card: avoid .head, .title and .line as types.
