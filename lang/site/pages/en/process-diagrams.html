Process and pipeline diagrams

A delivery pipeline, a data flow or a business process reads from left to right: stages are zones, steps are cards, and the arrows between them fork and join on their own.

How it is written

  • diagram(dir="right") lays the steps from left to right; cards of one stage stay in their zone, in the order you wrote them.
  • to(test lint) starts two steps in parallel: their arrows share a fork. Two steps that lead to the same next step meet there.
  • A state class marks an outcome: .ok, .warn, .error add a ring and a mark to the card (Rich cards).
  • A label on an arrow names the condition: to(prod) approved, to(screen).dashed no.

Loops and decisions

An arrow may go back to an earlier step: it is routed around the row instead of through it. Give a decision its own type and label its outgoing arrows:

card#decision.choice Decision
  to(send).ok yes
  to(screen).ko.dashed no

skemo draws boxes, not flowchart symbols: a decision is a card with its own colour, not a diamond.

Show it step by step

Add @1, @2… at the end of lines and the same file plays as an animation in HTML, GIF or animated PNG, one stage at a time (Step-by-step animations). Or keep the drawing still and let dots run along the arrows (Animated flows).
