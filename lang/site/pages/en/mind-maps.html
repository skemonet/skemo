Mind maps

A central idea, branches to the right and to the left. Write the ideas and the arrows from each one to its children; skemo balances the two sides and draws the branches.

Rules

  • diagram(layout="mindmap") enables the mode; root="id" picks the centre. By default the root is the first card that no arrow targets.
  • The root's children are spread between the right and the left, balancing the height of both sides; on each side, writing order reads top to bottom.
  • A node is centred on the block of its children; arrows of one parent share a trunk.
  • An arrow to a card already in the tree is drawn as an ordinary cross-link.
  • A card that cannot be reached from the root is placed under the map, with a warning.
  • Zones are not allowed; at= has no effect. Classes, themes and animation markers work as everywhere.

When to use it

Brainstorms, the outline of a talk or a document, the parts of a product. When the tree has a single direction (an organisation, a breakdown), an organisation chart reads better.
