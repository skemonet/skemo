Themes and contrast

Every diagram comes in four themes. Since types only define an accent, switching theme needs no change to your CSS.

The themes

ThemeUse
lightdefault; secondary texts, arrows and borders keep a contrast of at least 4.5:1
darkdark screens: only the base colours change, tints are recomputed from --c
printpaper, including black and white: white background, black ink, no shadow, darker fills and thicker strokes. Applied automatically when the HTML output is printed
autoHTML only: follows the reader's system setting. PNG and SVG render as light

Choose with diagram(theme="dark") or, overriding the file, skemo render --theme dark.

Dark

Print

The print theme may change dimensions slightly (thicker borders): the layout is computed for the theme you ask for.

Contrast

skemo check --contrast diagram.skemo           # reports texts below the WCAG threshold
skemo render --contrast high diagram.skemo     # darkens (or lightens) those texts, layout unchanged

The threshold is 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for text on an accent fill and for icons. A custom accent that is too light is reported, with the element and the measured ratio.

Never colour alone

Readers who do not see colours, and printers, still need to tell types apart: give types a --label, and arrows whose meaning matters a line style (.dashed, .dotted, .thick, .link).
