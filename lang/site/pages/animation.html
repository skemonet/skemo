Step-by-step animation

Markers at the end of a line make a diagram unfold step by step, for a talk or a tutorial.

Markers

WriteMeaning
@2appears at step 2 (effect fade; draw for an arrow)
@2:upappears at step 2 with the effect up; effects: fade up zoom draw
@2-4visible from step 2 to step 4 included, then hidden
@0-2 (preview)visible from the start to step 2 included, then hidden
@4:focus, @4-6:focushighlighted at step 4 (or from 4 to 6): the rest is dimmed

Markers go at the end of a card, zone, | or to(…) line, after at least one space, and are removed from the displayed text. Steps are ordered values, not a count: @2.5 slots between 2 and 3 without renumbering anything.

Replacing a line (preview)

@0-1 shows a line from the start and hides it after step 1; the next line appears at step 2. Lines that are never visible together share one place: the card is one line tall and the text changes in place.

Animating a chip (preview)

Markers at the end of a tag="…" value animate the chip alone: tag="8080 @1" shows the port at step 1. In HTML, this works with the band (default) and header looks.

Rules

  • At most one appearance marker per element; as many :focus as you like.
  • A child never appears before its parent nor outlives it (a card in a zone, a line in a card).
  • An arrow without a marker appears with the later of its two ends, drawn progressively.
  • Only the final tokens of a line are markers: meet @3 tomorrow stays text. \@2 writes a literal @2; admin@example.com needs no escaping.
  • An inconsistent marker (earlier than its parent, a range with no later step) gives a warning and is adjusted.

Outputs

CommandResult
skemo talk.skemo -o talk.htmlstandalone page with a step reader: arrow keys, space or PageDown
-o talk.gif, -o talk.apng, -o talk.webpanimation with the effects (--hold, --transition, --fps, --final-hold, --no-loop)
--step 2 -o s2.pngone state
--animated -o talk.pngone PNG per state (talk.00.png…)
--board -o board.pnga contact sheet of every state

In SVG, skemo animates nothing itself: it marks elements with data-step, data-fx, data-until and data-focus, for your own player. The root <svg> lists the steps in order in data-states (["1","2","2.5","3"]), so a player that only counts whole steps, such as reveal.js fragments, can map 2.5 to its own index.
