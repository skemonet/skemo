Cards and zones

Cards are the boxes of a diagram; zones group them. Both take a title, classes and a few attributes.

Keep titles short (under about 30 characters): a long title widens the card, while long lines wrap. Three or four lines per card read best.

A card without lines has its title centred; with lines, the title stays on the left above them. In a column of cards (a wrap="1" zone, a stack) where others have lines, a card without lines keeps the column's alignment.

Piles of cards (preview)

cards instead of card draws a pile: several instances of one thing (the tasks of a service, the machines of a group). It is a card in every respect: lines, arrows, classes, chip. count="4" says how many: up to three cards are drawn, and a ×4 badge sits on the pile's bottom-right corner, at the same place in every look. Without count, three cards and no badge. The cards behind belong to the card's box: arrows end on them and the top card keeps the width of its neighbours.

Zones

A zone encloses everything indented below it, with a margin and room for its title. Zones nest. A zone gives its accent colour to the untyped cards it contains, and an empty zone keeps a visible frame. With at="x,y", a zone can also take an imposed size="w,h". (Preview) tag="…" on a zone adds a pill after its title (zone#pods(tag="172.30.0.0/16") Pods), as on a card. .tag-left and .tag-right on a card or a zone put its badge before or after the name. A stylesheet may set a zone's title at the bottom (zone.svc > .title { top: auto; bottom: 12px }): the room for it is then kept below the cards.

Notes, chips and boxes

The theme provides secondary cards for annotations, and a frameless zone for captions:

ClassOnRendering
.notecardannotation: dashed border, tinted background, centred text, no badge
.chipcarda rounded pill on one line
.infocarda small, discreet box
.barezoneno frame; the title becomes a caption below, centred and in italics; without a title, nothing is drawn
.wide (preview)cardspans the width of its group, like a network or a bus bar; its arrows drop straight to it and end in a dot
.wide (preview)zonespans the width of its group too; its cards, on one row, share that width
.col-name (preview)card, zonea column: nodes of one .col-… class line up across the direction, from one zone to another (pods and services over the machine that hosts them)

They combine with your own types, and keep their plain style whatever the card look of the diagram. Use the .link arrow style to attach a note to a card.

Built-in types

The default theme ships a few network-flavoured types, handy for quick sketches: .t0 (red, "T0"), .t1 (orange), .t2 (blue), .t3 (grey) for cards, and .cloud, .dmz, .lan for zones. For anything else, define your own in a few lines of CSS.
