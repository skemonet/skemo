Chronological frieze

One central axis for long spans — decades, centuries, millennia — with eras as bands and events above and below. Dates before the common era are negative years.

Options

scalediagramyear · decade · century (default) · millennium; graduations keep at least 90 px apart

Dates

A year, negative before the common era, optionally followed by -MM or -MM-DD: date="-44-03-15". There is no year 0: 1 BC is followed by AD 1, and 0 is an error. As in timelines, end is included: end="476" runs to the end of 476. Two eras that share a boundary year (-509 → -27, then -27 → 476) overlap by less than 2 pixels and touch, on one row.

Placement

  • Eras stack in rows under the axis, longest first; a name too long for its band goes to its right.
  • Events go up or down, to the side that needs the lowest level, alternating on a tie; the stem stays on the exact date.
  • At century scale, centuries are labelled in Roman numerals, in the diagram's language.
  • Arrows are allowed, for the rare cause-and-effect link.

Grouping events (preview)

A zone frames its events and their stems, with its title on top. Its events all go to one side of the axis (above, unless another zone already holds that side over the same dates); the other events of that period go to the other side. Zones do not nest in a frieze.
