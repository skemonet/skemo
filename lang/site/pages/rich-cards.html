Rich cards, tables and panels (preview)

A card can say what it is, stress a word, carry a table or a finding; a diagram can have a legend, a footnote and panels set around the drawing. None of it needs a new tag: lines |, Markdown, classes and at=.

Category, emphasis and states

WriteEffect
label="RDS PostgreSQL 16.4"the card's category, a small line above its name; by default the type's --label; label="" shows none. tag= stays the chip on the right
**bold**, *italic*, `mono`Markdown emphasis inside a line or a table cell
==text==, ==.ok text==a mark: in red, or the colour of the line's state, or of the class written after ==
.ok, .warn, .erroron a card: a ring and a mark before the name; on a line (|.warn …): a mark and the state's colour; on a cell: the state's colour

Delimiters only count when they wrap a word: cron(0 2 * * ? *) and status == 200 stay as written, and _ is never emphasis. Write \*, \` or \== for the characters themselves.

Tables

A table is written as a Markdown table inside a card: a line that ends with | is a row, rows that follow each other are one table, and the card can hold lines of text before and after it. The spaces around a cell are trimmed, so a table aligned by any Markdown formatter stays valid.

WriteEffect
|---|---| after the first rowthe first row is a header; :---: centres a column, ---: aligns it right
|| (nothing in the cell)the cell on the left spans this column too; an empty cell is | |
^^the cell above spans this row too
|.error lambda |the cell takes the class (glued to the bar)
| alonean empty line; it also separates two tables

A table never wraps: its card grows as wide as it needs. Arrows end on the card, not on a row.

Legends and plain text

card.legend is a legend: each line |.class text shows a sample of the class it names, an arrow for a class used on arrows, a chip for a card type, a small frame for a zone type, the icon of a class that sets --icon, the mark for a state. A legend without lines lists by itself the classes of the diagram that have a --label in the stylesheet (arrow classes too: .edge.events { --label: "events"; }). card.text is plain text with no frame, for a note or a footnote.

Panels, legends and footnotes around the drawing

at= with a compass point docks a top-level card or zone outside the drawing, which is laid out as if it were absent: nothing in it moves.

WriteWhere
at="s", at="n"a band below (above) the drawing, over the whole width; several elements go side by side in writing order; a .wide one takes a row of its own (a footnote: card.text.wide(at="s"))
at="e", at="w"a column on the right (left) of the drawing
at="ne", at="nw", at="se", at="sw"in that column, at the top or the bottom: a legend at the top right is at="ne"

A docked zone is laid out on its own, like a small diagram. An arrow between a docked element and the rest of the drawing is not drawn (warning docked-arrow).

Subtitle and zone descriptions

Lines | under title are subtitle lines; |.pill lines are pills on the title's row. Lines under a zone are a description under its title (“3 availability zones”); the zone keeps room for them.
